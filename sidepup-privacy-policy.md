# Sidepup Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Sidepup is a Chrome extension that shows a small dog, a to-do list, a Pomodoro timer and optional stretch and water reminders on the pages you visit. This policy explains what it handles. The short version: Sidepup does not send your data anywhere.

## What Sidepup handles

- **Your tasks and settings.** The tasks you type, their reminder times, your timer and reminder settings, the position of the bar, and a short history of finished tasks and focus time. These are saved with Chrome's own storage (chrome.storage). If Chrome Sync is on for your Google account, Chrome syncs this data between your own browsers. That sync is done by Chrome and Google under their own terms, not by us.
- **Activity timing.** To work out when you have been working for a long stretch (for the stretch and water reminders), and to make the dog's eyes follow your cursor and react to typing, Sidepup notices that mouse, keyboard or scroll activity is happening and when. It does not record what you type outside Sidepup's own task box, which keys you press, the pages you visit, page titles or page content. This is processed on your device and only a time of last activity is kept.

## What Sidepup does not do

- It does not collect personal information, health, financial, authentication, location or communication data.
- It does not read, copy or transmit the content of the pages you visit, and it does not record your browsing history.
- It does not send any data to us or to any third party. Sidepup has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising.
- It does not sell or transfer user data, does not use it for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and does not use it to decide creditworthiness or for lending.
- It does not load or run remote code. Everything it needs, including its fonts, is inside the extension package.

## Permissions

- **storage**: to save your tasks, settings and history.
- **alarms**: to run the timer and reminders on time.
- **notifications**: to show a timer or reminder alert.
- **Access to http and https pages**: so the bar can appear on the pages you visit. It only draws the interface and reads nothing from the page.

## Your control

You can delete your tasks inside Sidepup at any time. Removing the extension deletes its stored data from your browser. You can turn off the stretch and water reminders in Settings, and hide the dog with Alt+Shift+H.
